# A referral pays the referrer only

A Referral pays the referrer when an Admin confirms the invited person's first paid subscription EFT. Starter pays 3 Credits, Pro pays 5, and Scale pays 10. The invited person gets none. A person can be referred once, a self-referral is refused, and a referrer can receive 5 rewards in a UTC month and 20 in total. Paying both sides, or paying at sign-up, was rejected: the reward would then fire before any payment existed. Changing a cap or a tier amount waits for approval.
